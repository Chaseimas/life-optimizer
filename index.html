      Rules

      • Start ONE product per week. Week 1: SA+moisturizer. Week 2: Niacinamide. Week 3: BP. Week 4: Azelaic. Week 5: Peel.
      • Shower within 30 min of gym. Sweat + bacteria = breakouts.
      • Clean gym clothes every session. Never re-wear.
      • Change sheets 2x/week. White pillowcase on BP nights (it bleaches).

      • 1 gallon water/day minimum. Critical with peptides.
